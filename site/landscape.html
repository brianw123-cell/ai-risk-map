Where AI's futures sit

Most arguments about AI come down to two questions: how fast it arrives, and whether control and benefits stay widely shared. Each of the eighteen scenarios is placed in the futures where it is most likely.

10 of 18 scenarios belong to futures where AI is widely shared. That includes the two misuse risks, because wide access is what makes misuse possible.
The concentration risks sit together at the bottom: job loss, energy demand, concentrated power. They don't depend on how fast AI arrives.
Only loss of control needs both fast change and lost control. It is the most debated scenario, and the narrowest.
