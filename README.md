# AI Scenario Map

A public map of the major ways AI could go, good and bad. For each scenario, the worried side and the optimistic side make their case in their own words, every probability is shown with its source and spread, and each scenario is sorted into what needs a policy response and what needs public communication.

Started 9 October 2026. Based on a proposal Emad Mostaque made on the Moonshots podcast (7 October 2026, around 49:14). The transcript is in the AI Ethics project: `../AI Ethics/transcript-moonshots-ep-risk-map.md`.

## Where things are

- `reports/AI scenario map framework.md` is the research report and proposed framework. Start here.
- `research_notes/AI scenario map framework/` holds the five sourced research notes behind it: risk catalogs, forecasting and disagreement, argument mapping and governance, benefits and scenario methods, funding and partners.

## The framework in one paragraph

Eighteen scenarios across eight domains, each with a good and a bad version, plus "AI disappoints" and an open "unknown and combined risks" entry. A simple two-axis view sits on top: how fast AI arrives, and whether control and benefits stay shared. Each scenario is a card with 17 fields, including both sides' cases signed off by that side, common ground, the questions that would change each side's mind, every published probability with its source and wording, and a four-step triage. The map never publishes a probability of its own.
