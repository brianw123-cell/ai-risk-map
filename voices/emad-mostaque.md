## Track record so far

Of the six factual claims checked from the Impact Theory episode: one confirmed (the escape), one partly confirmed (forecasting), two overstated (Navier–Stokes, the Anthropic numbers), one likely wrong in part (the AI-box example), one unverified (Medicare). Same pattern as the Ramez Naam check: the direction is usually right, the numbers often run ahead of the sources.
