# Voices

One file per person whose views shape the risk map, from both camps. The point is to treat each person's ideas as **claims to track**, not as conclusions to adopt, so trust can be earned and measured over time.

## What goes in a file

- **Who they are:** role, background, and anything they sell or build that their claims support. That is not a reason to dismiss them, only a reason to check the claims that help their business first.
- **Claims:** one row per distinct claim, each with the date, a link to the exact moment it was said, a short paraphrase (never a long transcript passage), its type, its check status, and the scenario cards it touches.
- **Predictions to score:** every claim with a date and a number goes here, with the date it can be checked. Over time this becomes the person's track record.

## Claim types

- **Prediction:** something will happen by a date. Gets scored.
- **Fact:** something already happened. Gets checked once.
- **Proposal:** what we should do. Goes to the triage step of the relevant card.
- **Values:** a judgment about what matters. Not checkable; shown as that person's position.

## Check status

- **Confirmed:** matches a primary or reputable source (linked).
- **Overstated:** the core is real but the claim goes further than the source.
- **Disputed:** credible sources disagree.
- **Unverified:** no source found yet.
- **Likely wrong:** a source contradicts it.

## How the map uses these files

1. **Before a card quotes someone**, the claim is checked here first.
2. **Named people become sign-off candidates** for the side of a card they argue.
3. **Confirmed facts that hit a card's signposts** trigger a card update.
4. **Predictions get scored** when their date arrives, for every voice equally, worried and hopeful alike.
